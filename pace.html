♥ HPBar — "over pace" cue

A gentle nudge on the 5-Hour bar's caption (e.g. "resets in 2h 45m · 20% over pace") when you've burned notably more than an even spend-down would predict by this point in the window. It's 5-Hour only, needs a ≥12-point lead, and is hidden when on/under pace or when the stronger "⚠ hits limit in ~Xm" warning is already shown. Mock data; no Keychain / network / Tauri.
